About

Four years shipping production systems, most of them for electric vehicles.

I started in 2022 as a web developer intern building client sites in PHP and WordPress. Today I lead full stack work at Codenius Tech, where I design and ship platforms that real charge-point operators and battery-swap stations run their business on.

My niche is the part most developers never touch: talking to hardware. I wrote the OCPP WebSocket server that EV chargers connect to, a Go service that streams school CCTV cameras to parents' phones, and the bridge that pulls punches from ZKTeco biometric devices. Around that I build the usual full stack: Laravel, NestJS and Node APIs, MySQL and PostgreSQL schemas, Flutter apps with Firebase push, Docker deploys. I also run AI models myself: offline speech recognition, a fine-tuned Qwen model, and a coding agent that works through tasks for my team.
